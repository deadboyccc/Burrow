Your own stealthy Telegram proxy.

burrow runs a Telegram MTProto proxy behind a real website with a real certificate, in hardened containers on a server you control. Telegram apps connect natively. Everyone else just finds a quiet personal blog.

Why burrow

Each piece was picked for safety, reliability and performance. Meet Digby: moles dig tunnels under walls, and this one wears flying goggles because your messages fly through.

🎭

Looks like a website

FakeTLS makes the handshake a TLS 1.3 hello to your own domain. Probes, scanners and browsers get a real site with a Let's Encrypt certificate.

🛡️

Hardened by default

Non-root, read-only, zero-capability containers. Images pinned by digest, dependencies by hash, secrets never in argv or logs.

🔗

Share with one link

A tg:// link and an offline QR code. burrow rotate revokes every link at once.

🩺

Self-healing

Restart policies, Docker live-restore and a watchdog that probes the proxy every five minutes and recreates it if it hangs.

⚡

Fast

mtg is a lean Go relay. BBR, fq and tuned buffers help on the lossy, long-distance links censored users live with.

🧪

Tested for real

261 automated tests, including live stacks that push real MTProto traffic to Telegram's data centres, and replay and probe attacks.

How it works

mtg owns port 443. A connection only reaches Telegram if its hello carries an HMAC made with your secret, has a fresh timestamp and has never been seen before. Everything else is spliced, byte for byte, to the decoy website.

Get started

About 20 minutes from nothing to your phone using your own proxy. The deployment guide covers every step.

Rent a small server, point a domain at it

1 vCPU, 1 GB RAM, Debian or Ubuntu. Add an A record such as notes.example.com → your server's IP. No domain? Use 203-0-113-7.sslip.io (your IP with dashes).

Run one command on the server

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/deadboyccc/Burrow/main/install.sh |
  sudo BURROW_DOMAIN=notes.example.com bash

Re-run it any time to upgrade; your secret is kept. Prefer your laptop? ./burrow deploy root@IP --domain … does the same over SSH.

Scan, connect, share

The installer ends by printing your links and a QR code. Later: /opt/burrow/burrow share shows them again, and /opt/burrow/burrow rotate revokes them all.
